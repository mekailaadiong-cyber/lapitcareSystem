Privacy Policy

Mock privacy notice for this front-end demonstration, updated October 19, 2026.

Information in this demo

Registration details and activity are stored in local storage in the browser you are using. The demo has no application server or database connection, so this information is not sent to a LapitCare backend.

Who can access local information

Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to inspect or change local demo data. Browser-only sign-in is not secure access control. Do not enter real personal, health, account, or clinic information.

Storage and removal

Demo data remains in the browser until site storage is cleared. Clearing it may remove registered demo accounts, profile edits, and appointment activity.

External services

Map tiles and map libraries may load from public third-party services when you use map features. This mock notice does not describe a production privacy program or replace a legally reviewed privacy policy.
