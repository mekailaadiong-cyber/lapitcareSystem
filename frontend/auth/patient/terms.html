Mock terms for this front-end demonstration, updated October 19, 2026.

Demo use

LapitCare is a fictional, browser-only demonstration. Clinic listings, accounts, schedules, and appointment activity may be sample data. Do not use this demo to request or provide real medical care.

Local account and appointment activity

Information entered in the demo is saved in this browser's local storage. It is not submitted to a LapitCare server, shared with a clinic, or synchronized to another device. Clearing browser site data may remove it.

No production services

Sign-in, verification, password recovery, booking, and notifications are simulated for demonstration purposes. They do not provide production security, email delivery, medical advice, or a confirmed real-world appointment.

Safe use

Use fictional names and contact details only. Do not enter passwords used for other services or any real patient, health, or clinic information.
